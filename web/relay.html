AI helper

With the AI helper, people can ask in plain words, like ?ask I have a small cut, how do I close it. It finds the right pages on this drive and, if you like, writes a short answer from the best one. It runs on this computer from the Lantern folder, with no internet and nothing installed into Windows.

How to ask

Send a direct message to the gateway node, from FieldMap's Ask Lantern screen or any Meshtastic app:

  • ?ask how do I make creek water safe asks the AI helper (if it's set up) to find the best pages and answer briefly. Plain questions with no ? go to the AI too.
  • ?s water purification searches everything on the drive and lists the top 3, like 1 Water purification [Wiki]. Plain words with no ? search too when the AI helper is off.
  • ?1 ?2 ?3 reads the start of that result.
  • ?more sends the next part (or the next 3 results after a search).
  • ?toc lists the sections. ?go 4 jumps to section 4.
  • ?help sends this list over the radio.

Set up the gateway node with the same private channel as your team (scan the channel QR code), and put 🤖 in its name so people can find it. Close the Meshtastic app or web flasher on this computer first, since only one program can use the USB port.
