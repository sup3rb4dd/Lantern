MOTION DETECTOR

Web flasher · firmware __VERSION__ for the ESP32 "Cheap Yellow Display" (ESP32-2432S028R) + HLK-LD2450 radar

This page needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (it uses Web Serial). Open it there to flash.

A people radar: the LD2450 24 GHz mmWave sensor tracks up to 3 moving people out to 6 m (20 ft), and the CYD shows them as blips on a motion-tracker fan with the distance to each. No WiFi. Without the sensor it runs a demo, so you can flash and try it now.

STEPS

  1. Close anything else using the CYD's port (Lantern, the Meshtastic app, other flashers).
  2. Plug the CYD into this computer with a USB data cable.
  3. Press Connect & flash, pick the port (often USB-SERIAL CH340 or CP210x), then Install.
  4. It restarts into the radar. With no sensor attached it says DEMO-NO SENSOR and shows made-up contacts.

WIRING THE SENSOR

  1. Power: sensor 5V to VIN and sensor GND to GND on the CYD's P1 connector (4 pins, labelled VIN, TX, RX, GND).
  2. Data: sensor TX to IO27 and sensor RX to IO22 on the CYD's CN1 connector. CN1's 3.3V pin isn't used; it can't power the sensor.
  3. Go by the labels printed on the boards, not wire colors. Both cables likely end in female jumpers, so join them with male-to-male jumper wires.
  4. Power it from USB (a phone charger or power bank is fine). The corner turns to SENSOR OK in green.

USING IT

Stand the sensor upright with its gold antenna side facing the room, about chest height. It sees 60° to each side.

Tap the screen's corners:

  1. Top-left: sound on/off (a ping on each new contact, through a speaker on the CYD's SPEAK connector).
  2. Top-right: mirror left/right, if people show up on the wrong side.
  3. Bottom-left: demo on/off.
  4. Bottom-right: range 6 m, 4 m or 3 m.

Settings are kept when it's unplugged. The CYD's LED turns amber while anyone is in view and red when someone is within 1.5 m (5 ft). The LD2450 tracks movement, so someone standing perfectly still fades out after a few seconds.
