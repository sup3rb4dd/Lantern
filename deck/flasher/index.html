LANTERN DECK

Web flasher · firmware __VERSION__ for the ESP32 "Cheap Yellow Display" (ESP32-2432S028R)

This page needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (it uses Web Serial). Open it there to flash.

Lantern Deck is a touchscreen status panel for Lantern Relay. It plugs into the PC running Lantern by USB and shows the team's messages, the nodes Relay has heard and Relay's status, with quick-reply buttons. No WiFi.

FLASH IT

Standard CYD
Most boards, with one micro-USB port. Start here.
Open this page over https to flash.
Inverted colors
If the standard version shows a white or light background instead of black (common on boards with two USB ports), flash this one instead.

STEPS

  1. Close Lantern (or turn Relay off), the Meshtastic app and any other flasher, so the USB port is free.
  2. Plug the CYD into this computer with a USB data cable.
  3. Press Connect & flash, pick the port (often USB-SERIAL CH340 or CP210x), then Install. Erasing first is fine; the Deck keeps no settings.
  4. When it finishes, the screen says Waiting for Lantern.

USE IT

  1. Plug the Deck into the PC that runs Lantern (1.11 or newer), and open Lantern.
  2. Turn Relay on (Relay page). Lantern finds the Deck on USB by itself within a few seconds. The Relay page shows Lantern Deck: Connected.
  3. Tabs along the bottom: MSG (team chat, questions to Relay and its answers), NODES (last heard, signal, battery), RELAY (status, questions today, AI helper).
  4. Quick replies: tap a button, then tap SEND? within 3 seconds. It goes out on Relay's reply channel. Change the buttons in Lantern under Relay › Settings.

Taps landing in the wrong spot? Some CYDs mount the touch panel differently; the touch mapping is in readTouch() in the firmware source.
